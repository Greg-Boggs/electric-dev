# 10. Blog post (template)

Topics come from the research step, which weights what AI answer engines cite over keyword volume.

**Purpose.** Answer one buyer question completely and plainly, so readers and answer engines both treat it as a source.

**Reader.** Defined per post.

**Primary search term.** One per post. Open (Q1).

**H1.** [The question, or the answer to it.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** One relevant link (a service page or Pricing). **Secondary.** Get a proposal at the end.

## Sections, in order

1. **Title and meta.**
   - Title, author name, date published, date updated.

2. **Short answer.**
   - The answer in two or three sentences at the top, before any background.

3. **Body.**
   - Headings phrased as the sub-questions readers ask.
   - Numbers cited inline with their source.

4. **Author.**
   - Who wrote it and why they know. Real person.

5. **Related.**
   - Two or three links to pages or posts.

6. **Closing call to action.**
   - One line and a button, not a banner.
