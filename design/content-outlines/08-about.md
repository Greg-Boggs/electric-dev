# 8. About

**Purpose.** Show the accountable people behind the company, and that we're local to Portland.

**Reader.** Anyone checking whether the company is real and trustworthy before they ask for a proposal.

**Primary search term.** Open (Q1).

**H1.** [Who we are, in a few words.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal.

## Sections, in order

1. **Why we started.**
   - The reason for the company, in the founder's own words. Short. Can mention, in a sentence, that modern tooling lets us offer published prices and fast support (Q3).

2. **Who's accountable.**
   - The people behind it: name, role, photo, one-paragraph background. Real people only.

3. **What we hold ourselves to.**
   - Three to five working principles, each backed by something on the site (published prices, approvals, reporting).

4. **Where we are.**
   - Portland, the area we serve (Q8), and how to reach us.

5. **Closing call to action.**

## Proof this page needs

- Founder bio and photo.
- Business legal name and address, as the owner wants them shown.

## Open questions

Q1, Q3, Q8.
