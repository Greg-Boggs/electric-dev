# 9. Get a proposal

**Purpose.** Turn interest into a conversation with the least effort, and tell the reader exactly what happens after they press the button. The form goes to Formspree, per the earlier decision.

**Reader.** Someone ready to talk, or nearly.

**Primary search term.** None; this page isn't built to rank.

**H1.** [Get a proposal.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** The form's submit button, labeled for what happens ("Get a proposal"), never "Submit".

## Sections, in order

1. **Intro.**
   - One sentence on what the reader will get back, and roughly when (open until set).

2. **The form.**
   - Fields from the style guide: name, work email, company size (10 to 49, 50 to 199, 200 to 499, 500 to 1,000), an open question for the reader, and "I'd rather talk on a call".
   - Possible additions, to keep or cut: number of computers, current IT setup, phone number. Fewer fields is better.

3. **What happens next.**
   - Numbered steps: we reply, a short call, a written proposal with a price. Timing open.

4. **Reassurance beside the form.**
   - No obligation, what we'll do with their details, link to the privacy policy, link to Pricing.

5. **Other ways to reach us.**
   - Email and phone, once they exist, with support hours (8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday).

6. **After submitting.**
   - A confirmation state that repeats the next steps. An error state that says what to do.

## Open questions

Q4, Q5, Q10, and the reply time.
