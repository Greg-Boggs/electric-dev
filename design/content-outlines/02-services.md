# 2. Services

**Purpose.** Show the full range of what we do on one page, so a buyer can see we cover their whole IT need, and route them to the right service page.

**Reader.** Someone checking whether we handle the thing they need, or comparing our range against their current provider.

**Primary search term.** Open (Q1).

**H1.** [Our IT services.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal. **Secondary.** See pricing.

## Sections, in order

1. **Intro.**
   - One or two sentences: most companies buy these together as Managed IT; any can be added on.

2. **Managed IT, featured.**
   - The all-in plan in a wider card: what it bundles, and a link to its page and to Pricing.

3. **All services.**
   - A grid of cards from the service list (Q2). Each: name, one sentence, link.

4. **What's included in every plan.**
   - The few things every customer gets regardless of plan, for example the help desk and reporting. Depends on Q4.

5. **Not sure what you need?**
   - A short prompt toward a call or a proposal.

6. **Closing call to action.**

## Open questions

Q1, Q2, Q4.
