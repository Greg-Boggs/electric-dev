# 4. Pricing

**Purpose.** Give the price, the unit it's charged by, and exactly what is and isn't included, so a buyer can estimate a monthly cost without talking to anyone. Published prices set us apart from most IT providers.

**Reader.** The person who signs off on the spend, and the one comparing us with a current provider's invoice.

**Primary search term.** Open (Q1).

**H1.** [Plain statement that prices are published.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal.

## Sections, in order

1. **Intro.**
   - One sentence on how pricing works (the unit, for example per user or per device: Q4).

2. **Plans.**
   - Two to four plan cards, featured plan in the middle.
   - Each card: plan name, price, unit, who it's for in one line, three to six included items, button.

3. **Cost estimate.**
   - A small calculator: number of users or devices in, monthly total out. Shows the math.

4. **Compare plans.**
   - Full table of what each plan includes, row by row, using the service list (Q2).

5. **Add-ons.**
   - Services priced separately, if any, such as out-of-hours support (Q7).

6. **What's not included.**
   - Hardware, software licenses, on-site visits (Q8), projects, out-of-hours work outside 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, if it's billed separately (Q7).

7. **One-time and other costs.**
   - Onboarding fee, minimums, contract length. Open (Q4).

8. **Pricing FAQ.**
   - Contract and cancellation, minimum size, adding and removing users, billing.

9. **Closing call to action.**
   - Ask for a proposal with an exact quote.
