# Content outlines: key pages

Outlines for the hired writer and the wireframes. They say what each page is for, the order of its sections, and what each section must get across. They are not copy.

**Positioning.** This is a traditional IT services website for a managed IT provider in Portland. It leads with the services companies buy, published prices and local, accountable people. The AI-enhanced way we deliver the work is the behind-the-scenes advantage. It gets explained where a buyer would look for it (one section of How we work, one section of Security and data handling, and a short mention where it explains a benefit), not built into the header, the home page or the page list.

- No line in these files is approved copy. Anything in `[square brackets]` is a placeholder that shows the job a line does, not its words.
- No claims, statistics, prices, response times or certifications appear here. Where a section needs one, the outline names the proof it needs and leaves it open.
- Anything that depends on the market research, or on a decision that hasn't been made, is listed as an open question, not guessed.
- Voice and tone follow the style guide's Voice section (`design/style-guide/index.html#voice`).

## Shared across every page

### Header

Brand on the left, five links, one primary button. To be confirmed in the site map step:

- Services
- Pricing
- Industries
- How we work
- About
- Button: Get a proposal

On phones the links move behind a "Menu" button (see the wireframes).

### Footer

Groups: Services, Pricing, Company (About, How we work, Industries, Blog, Contact), Trust (Security and data handling, Privacy, Accessibility). Plus the legal name, Portland location, support hours (8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday) and contact line once they exist.

### Proposed service list

A conventional managed IT line-up, to confirm in the site map step and against the research:

- Managed IT (the all-in monthly plan)
- Help desk and user support
- Cybersecurity
- Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
- Cloud and servers
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Device setup, onboarding and offboarding
- Network and Wi-Fi
- IT strategy and planning (vCIO)

### Calls to action

- Primary everywhere: Get a proposal.
- Secondary: See pricing.
- Never "Contact us for pricing" or "Learn more".

## Open questions that affect several pages

These block final copy, not the wireframes. Each page file repeats the ones that touch it.

- **Q1. Primary search term for each page.** Comes from the research step.
- **Q2. Final service list.** The list above is proposed. It drives Services, the service pages and the Pricing table.
- **Q3. How we describe the AI tooling.** How much to say, the words for it, and whether to name the model vendor (its third-party brand rules apply).
- **Q4. Plan names, units, prices and minimums.** Not set.
- **Q5. Which part of the 10 to 1,000 range to lead with.** The research may narrow it.
- **Q6. Which industries get pages.** From the research step.
- **Q7. Response times and out-of-hours terms.** Coverage hours are set: 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, with out-of-hours service available. (Pacific time is assumed; confirm.) Still open: response times, and how out-of-hours work is booked and priced.
- **Q8. On-site visits in the Portland area.** Whether they're offered, the area, and how they're priced.
- **Q9. Compliance status.** Which frameworks (for example SOC 2 or HIPAA) we can honestly speak to. The site claims none until a real basis exists.
- **Q10. How customers ask for help.** Email, phone, chat or a portal.
