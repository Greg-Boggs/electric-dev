# 1. Home

**Purpose.** In one screen, tell a buyer we're a Portland IT services company that runs their IT for a published monthly price. Show the services, give them reasons to trust us, and move them to a proposal or to pricing.

**Reader.** An owner, COO, office manager or IT lead at a company of 10 to 1,000 people who is unhappy with their current IT support or has none.

**Primary search term.** Open (Q1).

**H1.** [What the reader gets, in one sentence they could repeat to a colleague.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal. **Secondary.** See pricing.

## Sections, in order

1. **Hero.**
   - Managed IT for companies like theirs, in plain words.
   - One supporting sentence: what we take off their plate, and that prices are published.
   - Primary and secondary buttons.

2. **Services.**
   - Six to nine cards from the service list, each linking to its page. Link to all services.

3. **Why companies choose us.**
   - Three or four reasons, each a short heading and a sentence. Candidates: published prices, local people in Portland, fast fixes, and one plain line on the tooling (an AI-enhanced service desk is why we can [benefit]). Which reasons, and the wording, depend on Q3 and research.
   - No figures unless measured and cited.

4. **How getting started works.**
   - Three steps: a call, a written proposal with a price, onboarding.
   - Link: How we work.

5. **Pricing preview.**
   - Prices are published; the unit and the starting point.
   - Link: See pricing. Figures depend on Q4.

6. **Industries we serve.**
   - Cards linking to industry pages (Q6).

7. **Proof.**
   - Customer logos or quotes, only with permission. Leave out until real ones exist.

8. **Short FAQ.**
   - Four to six questions a buyer asks first, such as whether there's a contract, whether we come on site, what hours we cover (8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, with out-of-hours service available), and how fast we respond (Q7). Answers link deeper.

9. **Closing call to action.**
   - One sentence on what happens after they ask for a proposal. Get a proposal button.

## Proof this page needs

- Any comparison or speed figures, measured and cited.
- Customer names or quotes, with permission.
