# 7. Industry page (template)

One outline for pages aimed at an industry (for example professional services or healthcare) or a situation (for example switching IT providers). Which ones get pages is Q6.

**Purpose.** Show we understand the reader's industry, its software and its rules, and explain what we do for companies like theirs.

**Reader.** Defined per page.

**Primary search term.** One per page. Open (Q1).

**H1.** [IT support for this industry, in the reader's words.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal. **Secondary.** See pricing.

## Sections, in order

1. **Hero.**
   - Name the industry and what we offer it.

2. **Common IT problems in this industry.**
   - Three or four, stated calmly.

3. **What we do for you.**
   - The services that matter most here, linking to their pages.

4. **Software and systems we support.**
   - The tools this industry runs on. Only ones we really support.

5. **Compliance and requirements.**
   - Industry rules, only where we can honestly speak to them (Q9).

6. **Switching to us.**
   - The steps to move from a current provider, and what we need.

7. **Price example.**
   - A worked example for a typical company in this industry, once prices exist (Q4).

8. **FAQ.**

9. **Closing call to action.**

## Proof this page needs

- A real customer story in this industry, later, with permission.
