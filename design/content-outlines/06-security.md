# 6. Security and data handling

**Purpose.** The trust page every IT provider needs: how we protect customers' systems, how our access to them is controlled, and what happens to their data. Linked from the footer and from the Cybersecurity service page, not the header. Precise and sober. No fear selling.

**Reader.** The IT lead, security owner or compliance person who has to approve the vendor.

**Primary search term.** Open (Q1).

**H1.** [How we protect your systems and data.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal. **Secondary.** Request the security document, if one exists.

## Sections, in order

1. **Summary.**
   - Three to five short statements a reader can repeat.

2. **How we access your systems.**
   - The remote management tools we use, how access is secured (for example MFA and least privilege), who on our team has access, and how you revoke it.

3. **Changes and approvals.**
   - Which changes need your approval, and how every action on your systems is logged.

4. **Your data.**
   - What we store, where, for how long, and who our vendors are.

5. **AI tools.**
   - One short section: where AI is used in our service desk, what it can and can't do on your systems, that a person approves changes, and whether any customer data is used to train models. Detail depends on Q3 and the operating rules.

6. **Our own security practices.**
   - How we secure our company: staff devices, training, background checks, if true.

7. **Incidents.**
   - What we do if something goes wrong, and how and when you're told.

8. **Vendors we rely on.**
   - Third parties that handle customer data, with what each does.

9. **Compliance.**
   - Only what's true today (Q9). Leave out if nothing yet.

10. **Report a security issue.**

11. **Closing call to action.**

## Proof this page needs

- Written access and approval rules.
- Data retention details and vendor list.
- Any audit or certification (Q9).

## Open questions

Q1, Q3, Q9.
