# 3. Service page (template)

One outline used for every service, including Managed IT and Cybersecurity. The services themselves come from Q2.

**Purpose.** Explain one service in enough depth that a buyer knows what it covers, how we deliver it, what it doesn't cover and what it costs.

**Reader.** Someone who searched for this specific service or problem, or clicked through from Home or Services.

**Primary search term.** One per service. Open (Q1).

**H1.** [The service, named the way customers say it.] Placeholder.

**Primary action.** Get a proposal. **Secondary.** See pricing.

## Sections, in order

1. **Hero.**
   - What this service takes off the reader's plate, in one sentence. Buttons.

2. **Signs you need this.**
   - Three or four common problems, in the customer's words.

3. **What's included.**
   - The tasks and deliverables under this service, as a checklist.

4. **How we deliver it.**
   - Three or four steps, from setup to ongoing work. Where the tooling makes a real difference to this service, one sentence on it, linking to How we work.

5. **Limits.**
   - What this service doesn't cover, and where that goes instead.

6. **Price.**
   - Which plans include it, or its own price if it has one (Q4). Link to Pricing.

7. **FAQ.**
   - Three to five questions specific to this service.

8. **Related services.**
   - Two or three cards.

9. **Closing call to action.**

## Proof this page needs

- Real process detail for each service.
- Later: a short customer story, with permission.
