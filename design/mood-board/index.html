What I need from you

Pick a direction, or tell me which pieces to mix. A few things worth reacting to:

  • Dark or light as the main look. Dark tells the AI story faster. Light is easier to sell to finance and compliance buyers.
  • How visible the AI should be. A shows the machine working. C puts the people approving the work first. B sits in between.
  • Whether the terminal "fix log" should be a signature element on the real site. I think it's our best proof, whichever direction we pick.

Numbers in the samples (fix times, machine counts, the $135 price) are made up for layout. None of them are claims yet.
