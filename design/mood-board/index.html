Palette

Amber is never text on a light background. On light surfaces it's a fill with navy text on top (8.8:1), and for amber-colored words we use Beacon ink.

Steady hands on every machine.

A serif headline reads as established and grown up, which helps when a CFO is comparing us with an MSP that has been around for 20 years. Plex Sans keeps body copy and tables plain and easy to scan. Semibold 600 handles headings, labels and emphasis. Regular 400 does everything else.
