    **What you bring**


    - One or more years of help desk or desktop support experience

    - Patience with people who aren''t technical, and the habit of writing things down

    - A driver''s license and a way to get to client offices around the Portland metro area

  body: 'You own the systems behind the service desk: networks, servers, cloud tenants and the security controls that keep our clients safe. You''ll lead projects from plan to handover.

    - Design and run projects such as office moves, network refreshes and cloud migrations

    - Manage firewalls, switches, Wi-Fi, backups, endpoint security and identity

    - Take escalations from the service desk and help technicians grow their skills


    **What you bring**


    - Three or more years in a systems or network role, ideally at a managed service provider

    - Strong Microsoft 365 and Entra ID skills, plus experience with networking and firewalls

    - Experience with backup, endpoint protection and multi-factor authentication rollouts

    - Clear writing for change plans and client-facing summaries


    Schedule: Monday to Friday, within our 8am to 6pm hours, with a share of the out-of-hours rota and planned evening work for major changes.'
