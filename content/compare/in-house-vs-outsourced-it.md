## What an in-house hire really costs

A salary is only part of it. Add benefits, payroll taxes, training, and the security and management tools one person needs to do the job properly. Then add the cost of nobody covering when they're on vacation or sick.

## What outsourcing gets you

A managed IT provider gives you a team instead of a person: help desk, security monitoring, network and cloud skills, and someone to call at 7am on a Monday. You pay a predictable monthly fee per user.

## When in-house makes sense

If you have specialized systems that need someone on site every day, an in-house person is valuable. Many larger companies keep that person and add a provider for security, after-hours cover and projects. That's [co-managed IT](/compare/co-managed-vs-fully-managed/).
