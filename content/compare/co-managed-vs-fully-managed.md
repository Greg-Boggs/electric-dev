## Fully managed

The provider is your IT department. They handle support requests, security, devices, accounts, backups and planning. This fits companies without IT staff.

## Co-managed

Your IT lead stays in charge. The provider adds tools, people and specialist skills where your team is stretched. You agree up front who owns what, so nothing falls between you.

## How to choose

Count the hours your team spends on routine tickets, and ask what they'd do with that time back. If the answer is "the projects we never get to", co-managed is probably the right fit.
