## Per user

You pay a monthly fee for each person, covering their computers, accounts and support. It's easy to budget and matches how most offices work now, with people on several devices.

## Per device

You pay for each computer, server or network device. It can work for shop floors or shared workstations, but costs climb as people add laptops and phones.

## Hourly

You pay when something breaks. It looks cheap until a bad month, and it rewards the provider for problems rather than preventing them. We use hourly rates only for projects and work outside a plan. See our [pricing](/pricing/).
