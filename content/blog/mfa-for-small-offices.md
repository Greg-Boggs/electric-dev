## Why it matters

Most break-ins at small companies start with a stolen password. MFA means a stolen password alone isn't enough.

## Where to start

Turn it on for email first, then remote access, then admin accounts. Use an authenticator app rather than text messages where you can.

## Bring people along

Tell staff why, give them a date, and have someone available on the first morning to help. Most people are set up in five minutes.
