## Pick a file and restore it

Choose a real file from last week and restore it somewhere safe. Time how long it takes.

## Try something bigger

Once a quarter, restore a whole mailbox or a computer. That's where problems hide.

## Write it down

Record what you tested, when, and what you found. Your insurer will ask, and you'll want the answer ready.
