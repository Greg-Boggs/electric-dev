## What we collect

When you fill in our proposal form or email us, we collect what you send: your name, work email, company, phone number and message. Our website keeps basic, anonymous visit statistics. We don't use advertising cookies.

## How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, prepare a proposal and provide our services. We don't sell your information or share it for marketing.

## Who else handles it

A small number of service providers help us run the business, such as our form, email and ticketing providers. They handle your data only to provide that service to us.

## Customer systems

Data we can access while supporting your company stays yours. Our [security and data handling](/security/) page explains how we protect it and what happens when a contract ends.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email privacy@electricdev.net and we'll reply within 30 days.
