## Before someone starts

- Order and set up their computer
- Create their email and core accounts, with MFA
- Add them to the right groups, shared drives and software
- Send login instructions to their manager

## When someone leaves

- Disable their accounts on their last day
- Transfer their files and email to their manager
- Remove them from shared accounts and change any shared passwords
- Collect and wipe their devices

## Making it routine

Put both checklists in a form your managers fill in, so nothing depends on memory. We run both as part of every plan. See [onboarding and offboarding](/services/device-management/).
