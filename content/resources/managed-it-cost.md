## Typical price ranges

Remote-only help desk and basic security typically starts around $100 to $125 per user per month. Fully managed IT with on-site support, backup and training usually runs $150 to $175. Plans with round-the-clock security monitoring generally cost $200 or more.

## What changes the price

Your number of users, how much on-site help you need, your compliance requirements, and how many servers and network devices you run. Licenses such as Microsoft 365 are usually extra.

## One-time costs

Expect an onboarding fee, often equal to one month of service, to document your systems and bring them up to standard.
