## Before you give notice

Check your current contract for notice periods and what they're required to hand over. Make a list of every system: accounts, devices, network equipment, backups and software vendors.

## During the handover

Your new provider should collect admin access, document everything, install their tools and check your security settings. Ask for a written handover checklist.

## After cut-over

Confirm the old provider's access is removed, and that backups and monitoring are running under the new provider. Then tell your staff how to get help. Our [how we work](/how-we-work/) page shows our onboarding steps.
