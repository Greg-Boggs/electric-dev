## The controls insurers ask about

- Multi-factor authentication on email, remote access and admin accounts
- Endpoint detection and response (EDR) on every computer
- Backups that are separate from your network and tested
- Regular security awareness training and phishing tests
- Email filtering for phishing and malicious attachments
- Timely patching of operating systems and software

## How to show them

Insurers want evidence, not just a yes. Keep reports showing MFA coverage, EDR status, backup test results and training completion.
