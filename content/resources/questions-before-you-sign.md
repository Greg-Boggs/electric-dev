## About the price

What exactly is included per user? What's billed extra? Are licenses marked up? Is there an onboarding fee?

## About support

What are the hours? How fast do they respond, and is that in writing? Will you have a named contact? Do they come on site?

## About security

What protection is included? Who's watching alerts at night? How are backups tested?

## About leaving

How long is the contract, and is there an early exit? Will you get every password and document when you go? A good provider answers all of this before you ask.
