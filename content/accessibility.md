## Our goal

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we've done

The site works with a keyboard and screen readers, uses readable color contrast, respects reduced-motion settings and scales with your browser's text size.

## Tell us about problems

If something doesn't work for you, email hello@electricdev.net or call (503) 555-0142. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll fix it or get you the information another way.
