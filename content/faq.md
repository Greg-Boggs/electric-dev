blocks:
- type: faq
  heading: Getting started
  items:
  - q: What happens after I ask for a proposal?
    a: We reply to set up a short call, learn how your IT is set up today, and send a written proposal with a price. No obligation.
  - q: What size companies do you work with?
    a: Companies of 10 to 1,000 employees. Plans have a 10-user minimum.
  - q: How long does onboarding take?
    a: Usually two to four weeks, depending on your size. See [how we work](/how-we-work/).
- type: faq
  heading: Support
  items:
  - q: What hours do you cover?
    a: Our support desk is open 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday. Out-of-hours service is available when something can't wait, and it's included in the Secure plan. See [coverage and hours](/coverage/).
  - q: Do you come on site?
    a: Yes. The Business and Secure plans include on-site support in the Portland metro area. On Essentials, on-site visits are billed at $150 per hour.
  - q: How do my employees ask for help?
    a: By email at support@electricdev.net, by phone at (503) 555-0142, or through our support portal.
  tone: tint
- type: faq
  heading: Pricing and contracts
  items:
  - q: Is there a contract?
    a: Yes, a 12-month agreement, with an early exit window of 90 days if we're not the right fit. See [pricing](/pricing/) for the details.
  - q: What does onboarding cost?
    a: A one-time fee equal to one month of service.
  - q: Are licenses included?
    a: Microsoft 365 and other licenses are passed through at cost.
- type: faq
  heading: Security
  items:
  - q: Do you use AI?
    a: Yes, behind the scenes. Our service desk uses AI tools to diagnose routine problems faster, and our technicians approve changes and handle anything the tools can't. [How we work](/how-we-work/#tools) explains it.
  - q: Can we revoke your access?
    a: Yes, at any time. See [security and data handling](/security/).
  tone: tint
- type: cta
  heading: Ready to see what your IT would cost?
  text: Tell us about your company and we'll send a written proposal with a price.
